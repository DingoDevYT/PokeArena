# S2: seamless interiors at true size (Landon, 2026-10-15)

"Interiors act as their own worlds and are far larger. I'd much prefer the buildings are their ACTUAL size, which means towns and cities get to be an accurate size, and when a player walks in, the interior is a seamless transition."

## Rules
- Every enterable building's interior lives INSIDE its footprint in the overworld, at the same scale as the outside. No separate room maps, no fade to black, no teleport. (Caves and the Route 4 underground stay separate maps: they are natural, not buildings.)
- A building is bigger on the map if its interior needs the room: size the building to the interior, not the other way round. Towns grow to fit (Gen 4 style, lived-in layout, docs/MAPDESIGN.md).
- Building data: each building instance in tools/adv-towns/*.json carries `interior: { floor, walls, props: [[name, dx, dy]], npcs: [...], exits: [[dx, dy]] }` in tile offsets from the footprint origin, plus `floors` for multi-storey buildings (each storey is its own layer over the same footprint; stairs swap the visible storey).
- Rendering: outside, the building draws as its normal exterior sprite. When the player steps through the door (crosses into the footprint), the roof and front wall fade out in 3 hard steps (pixel art rule: no smooth fades) over 0.2 s, revealing the interior layer drawn in place: floor tiles, inner walls along the footprint edges (back wall drawn upright like Gen 4 interiors), furniture and NPCs, all depth-sorted with the player. The outside world stays visible but darkens a notch (stepped). Walking out reverses it.
- Collision: the footprint perimeter is wall except at exits; inside, furniture props have their own solid rects. The exterior's solid rect stops applying to the inside of the footprint while you are in it.
- Other players and NPCs: everyone shares one world; a player inside a building is drawn only when the viewer is inside the same building or the building is cut away for them (otherwise hidden behind the roof). Online sync unchanged except `room` becomes a building id.
- Art: interiors use the interior sheet (assets/adv/interior.png, clean 2x art) and community interior sets (density 0.5, drawn 1:1 outdoors after the 2x world scale, so pixels match sprites). Exterior building sprites must have a matching footprint; if a building's exterior art is too small for its interior, use a bigger real building sprite (never script-drawn, docs/TILE-QA.md).
- Migration: convert every existing room in data/adv/rooms/ (Goldacre, Salt Bay, Longshore, Tradeway, Pinegrove, Spindlewood, Sporehaven) into in-footprint interiors with the same NPCs, scripts (nurse, PC, clerk, gym leaders), trainers and lines; delete the room maps and door warps once the in-place version passes the checks.
- Checks: adv-check asserts each building's interior fits its footprint, every exit reachable from the door, NPC scripts work in place, gym fights still run (the world battle box is fine inside a big building; small interiors push the fight box out through the door into the street, which is fine).
- Screenshots per migrated building: outside, the cutaway moment, inside.

## S2 as built (engine + Goldacre)
- Data: `tools/adv-towns/G7.json` lists a building as `[name, doorx, doory, id, {w, d, ex}]` (footprint tiles, exit column); its inside is in `G7-in.json` (written by tools/adv-build-rooms.mjs, which owns the layouts) and the generator emits `world.buildings`. The generator asserts the footprint is level, dry and unpaved. A building with no 5th element is a legacy room warp, so unmigrated towns still work.
- Engine (src/adv-world.js): `ADV.blds`, `bldAt`, `bldHit` (perimeter walls except the exit, furniture solids), `cutTick` (3 hard steps in `FADE` 0.2 s), `bldNpcs`. The exterior prop carries `p.b`, so its solid rect is off. `scene.room` is `{id: building id, door: {town}, d: {bld, mach}}` while inside; the net `room` is the building id.
- Render (src/adv-render.js `cutaway`): floor, wall tops, back wall and furniture are drawn in place at the world scale (interior sheet 1:1); the exterior fades by alpha steps and the outside dims one notch through an evenodd veil.
- Goldacre: its 11 rooms and door warps are gone (48 rooms remain); src/adv-scale.js is folded into src/adv-save.js. Shots: docs/shots/s2-*.jpg.
- Phase 2: every other town's rooms are buildings too (Salt Bay, Longshore, Tradeway, Pinegrove, Spindlewood, Sporehaven: 58 buildings in all). tools/adv-build-rooms.mjs deals each old room's people, lines, scripts and trainers into a furnished interior (templates for Center, Mart, houses, hall, market, the two gyms) and writes `<code>-in.json`; the town JSON names the sprite and footprint. Only the Route 4 cave is still a room map (live, two door warps); the cave and `ADVS.atDoor` (Center heal in towns with no Center building yet) are all that is left of the old warp path.
