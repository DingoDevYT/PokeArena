# PokeArena: Adventure (working title)

Design decisions locked with Landon on 2026-10-14. Nothing is built yet.

## Where it lives
- Inside the PokeArena repo. Title menu: Adventure / Arena. Arena is unchanged.
- Arena can load the team you caught in Adventure from your save file.
- Silver Gallium: art and assets ONLY (tile sheets, entity sheets, DUMP, SFX). None of its code or mechanics.

## Look
- Gen 4 base, modern polish. Flat 3/4 top-down, crisp 1:1 pixels, no perspective tilt.
- Real elevation: cliffs, bridges, stairs you walk over and under. Accurate depth sorting, cast shadows, lighting.
- One seamless outdoor overworld streamed in chunks. Interiors are separate rooms with a fade.
- Maps: Claude builds them. No user-facing editor.
- Art sources: official Gen 4 rips plus community Gen 4 packs whose authors allow use (credits screen).

## Sprites
- Pokemon: PMD SpriteCollab everywhere (same as Arena). The sprite never swaps between world and fight.
- Trainers: Gen 4 HGSS/Platinum overworlds. Diagonal frames GENERATED by a repeatable tool so movement is true 8-way.
- Player: outfit customiser. 2-4 base bodies cut into layers (skin, hair, top, bottom, hat, bag), parts taken from existing trainer sprites and palette-recoloured. Diagonals generated per layer.

## Encounters and battles
- Your lead Pokemon always follows you. When a fight starts it steps forward and you take control of it.
- Wild mons are mostly visible and roaming. Some hide in tall grass and ambush.
- No ring: the open map is the arena. Real trees, rocks and water are obstacles. Fleeing = get far enough away.
- Pile-ons: wild mons of the SAME species (groups, flocks) join a fight in progress. Crowded areas are risky but good for grinding fodder.
- You control ONE partner only, switching with the wheel.
- Catching: partner button, auto-throw, classic catch formula (no aiming).
- NPC trainers: route trainers spot you (line of sight); gym trainers and rivals are talk-to-start. No fleeing trainer fights.
- Items mid-fight: quick wheel, shared cooldown, trainer briefly busy.
- Blackout: wake at the last Pokemon Center, lose some money.
- Wild levels fixed per area.
- Moves on the banned list are skipped from learnsets until each gets a real-time design.

## Content and systems (first slice)
- One big route plus a town, in Landon's region (he is sending the region).
- Each player picks their own hometown at the start. Wild levels and the badge system are tied to this (details coming with the region file). Spawn and blackout point default to the hometown.
- Story-driven from the start (needs his lore).
- Dex: any generation, picked per route to fit the region. Widen the bake scripts as needed.
- Pokemon Center, Mart, money, PC boxes, evolution and level-up moves, TMs, field abilities as key items or partner abilities (no HM slaves).
- Server clock (shared in-game day) plus per-zone weather that carries into fights. Spawns vary by time.
- Music: official HGSS/DPPt OST. Battle music cross-fades in (no screen transition).
- PC first (keyboard/mouse, controller). Phone touch controls later.

## Saves
- Manual only: Save downloads a .sav file, Load opens one. No browser storage.
- AES encryption plus a tamper check that rejects edited files. Determined players can still dig out the key.

## Multiplayer
- Max 16 players per server.
- Host owns the shared world (wild spawns, NPCs, positions). Each battle runs on the machine of the player who started it; co-op joiners connect to them.
- If the host leaves, another player takes over the world (state mirrored to a backup peer).
- Joining: auto-join the fullest server with room, server list, join a friend, offline/solo.
- Social: see each other, co-op wild fights (only the starter can catch), PvP challenge on the map, face-to-face trading, text chat.

- Balance pass (2026-11-05, from a sim and Monte Carlo analysis): EXP per fight falls with badges, ADVP.expPace = max(0.3, 0.85 / (1 + 0.2 x badges)), so a player who fights the trainers on the way arrives within about a level of each leader's ace (a flat rate always drifts ahead; the old 1.5 had also collided with ADVP.PACE, the speeds, and made every award NaN). Mons behind your best get up to +90% (ADVP.catchUp). Wild and trainer movesets are the three best attacks plus a status move, without an evolved form's strong level 1 moves; a new starter gets its level 8 kit plus its next attack. Adventure HP curve capped at 1.0 (Arena keeps 1.6), wild packs at most 3 per fight, tall grass ambush 1.2% per 32 px with 20 s grace after a fight, leaders' banned moves backfilled with real attacks, NEXT GOAL says train to the ace's level, blackout costs a quarter of your money (at most 2000), prizes x0.75 from 4 badges.
